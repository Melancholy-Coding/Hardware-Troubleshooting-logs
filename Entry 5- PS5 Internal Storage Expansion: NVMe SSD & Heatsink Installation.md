# Entry 05 — PS5 Internal Storage Expansion: NVMe SSD & Heatsink Installation
**Log Type:** `[Retroactive]`  
**Date:** August 2026  
**System / Hardware Profile:** Sony PlayStation 5 Console, WD_BLACK SN770 1TB NVMe SSD (PCIe Gen 4, M.2 2280), EGSMTPC Double-Sided Aluminum M.2 Heatsink with Thermal Pads  
**Tools / Software:** Screwdriver, PS5 system software (drive detection)  
**Category:** Storage Expansion / Thermal Management / Hardware Installation

---

## 1. Objective & Purpose
The PS5's internal storage was full and more space was needed for additional game installs. Expand storage by installing an M.2 NVMe SSD with a heatsink in the console's expansion slot.
## 2. Pre-Installation Checks
* **Drive Compatibility:** Verified the SSD met Sony's requirements: PCIe Gen 4 NVMe in the M.2 2280 form factor.
* **Cooling Requirement:** Sony requires a heatsink or other cooling structure on M.2 drives because NVMe SSDs generate significant heat under sustained load. Sourced a compatible double-sided aluminum heatsink with thermal pads rated for PS5 and M.2 2280 drives.
## 3. Installation Procedure
1. **Heatsink Prep:** Cleaned the SSD, then applied the two thermal pads, one on the top and one on the bottom.
2. **Heatsink Assembly:** Screwed the double-sided heatsink enclosure together around the SSD and adjusted its position so it stayed in contact with the drive for heat transfer without blocking the drive's connector or the M.2 slot.
3. **Physical Install:** Removed the PS5 side panel and installed the SSD with the heatsink into the expansion slot.
4. **Power-On:** Powered the console back on.
## 4. Verification & Final Results
* **Detection:** The PS5 detected the SSD immediately on power-on, and no manual formatting steps were needed.
* **Performance Note:** The SN770's rated sequential read speed is slightly below Sony's recommended 5,500 MB/s, but it has handled game storage without issues since installation.
* **Result:** Usable game storage was expanded successfully.
## 5. Prevention & Best Practices
* **Check Specs First:** Verify the drive meets Sony's requirements (PCIe Gen 4 NVMe, supported M.2 size, ideally 5,500 MB/s or faster read speed) before buying.
* **Cooling:** Always install a heatsink or cooling structure, and confirm it fits without blocking the slot.
