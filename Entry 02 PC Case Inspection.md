# Entry 02 — Pre-Build Inspection: Motherboard Standoff Alignment

**Log Type:** `[Retroactive]`  
**Date:** January 2026  
**System Profile:** Custom Desktop Build ([Case Model / Motherboard Model])  
**Category:** Hardware Installation / Preventive Compatibility Check  

---

## 1. Purpose & Objective
Prior to installing the motherboard and core components into a custom desktop chassis, perform a rigorous physical compatibility and dry-fit inspection to prevent short circuits, component stress, or clearance failures during assembly.

## 2. Inspection Process
1. **Standoff Mapping:** Aligned the motherboard's mounting hole layout against the pre-installed standoff positions in the PC chassis.
2. **Visual Board Inspection:** Inspected the motherboard PCB and traces around the CPU socket, Voltage Regulator Modules (VRMs), and mounting points for any manufacturing defects or transit damage.
3. **Thermal Clearance Check:** Evaluated the CPU cooler mounting hardware dimensions against nearby VRM heatsinks and RAM slot clearances.
4. **Power & Thermal Compatibility:** Verified that the planned components operated safely within the power supply unit (PSU) and cooling subsystem specifications.
5. **Dry-Fit Validation:** Temporarily placed the motherboard and cooler into the chassis without hardware to confirm physical alignment.

## 3. Findings & Risk Identification
Several case standoffs did not align with the motherboard's mounting holes. 

> **Technical Note:** An unaligned standoff left touching the underside of a live motherboard PCB can create an electrical bridge, causing a direct short circuit that can permanently damage the motherboard or power supply.

## 4. Corrective Action & Remediation
1. Unscrewed and removed all extra, misaligned standoffs from the chassis floor.
2. Re-configured the remaining standoffs to precisely match the motherboard's specific form-factor mounting pattern.
3. Performed a secondary dry fit to ensure complete physical clearance.

## 5. Verification & Final Results
* The motherboard sat flush and secured correctly in the chassis with zero standoff interference.
* Cooler mounting hardware aligned seamlessly, confirming the build environment was safe for final component installation.

## 6. Prevention & Best Practices
* **Always Dry-Fit:** Never mount a motherboard blindly; always verify standoff positions first to avoid catastrophic short circuits on initial power-up.
* **Component Planning:** Double-check form-factor compatibility (e.g., ATX vs. Micro-ATX) before starting physical builds.
